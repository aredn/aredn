AI Submission Guidelines
===

* Any submission must start with a human written explaination of the code or bug report.
* We do not accept code or bug reports only written by AI. Feel free to use AI to help, but it must be clear to us that a human understands. AI can be wrong and we want you to find that, not us.
* Any AI content must include the specific information about the AI used to generate it (product, model, etc.).
* We should not be the first people to read and understand your submission.
* Any discussion about a submission must be with the human submitter and not the AI.
